### The real bug: exit price reconciliation is wrong

The discrepancy is **bidirectional** (trade 1 understates loss, trade 2 overstates it), which rules out a constant-offset cause (tick rounding, timezone, fee bundling). The signature points at the engine recording **the price it observed at "close decision" time** rather than **the actual fill-confirmation price from the broker stream**.

Evidence:
- `DebugTrades.ExitPrice` = 29606.00 for trade 1, but the broker tape shows the actual sell fill at 29591.50.
- The `Closed` row in `DebugTradeActions` (14:00:49.194Z) carries `Price=29606.00, Reason='P&L Close (engine-derived from PnL)'`. The "engine-derived" phrasing confirms the engine computed the exit price from its internal state rather than waiting for the broker's fill confirmation.
- The actual `EntryFilled` event for trade 1 *did* use a broker-confirmed price (29616.50), which matches the broker tape exactly. So the entry-side reconciliation works; only the exit side is broken.

### Why this matters beyond accuracy

- **Phased-stop / scoring decisions made from `UnrealisedPnlDollars` are based on the wrong price.** If `BarCollectionService` or `TradeRecordService` feeds the engine's last-known tick into the P&L calc, then phase transitions (Initial → BE → ProfitLock) are firing against the engine's view, not the broker's view.
- **Daily-loss kill switches, R-multiple stats, win-rate tracking, and ML training data all consume `LiveTradeRecords.PnL`.** Trade 1's recorded loss is 42% of the actual broker loss. Aggregated across a week of trades, that's a meaningful drift in every downstream metric.
- **The exit price the engine uses is also the price `ExitReason='P&L Close'` evaluated against.** If the engine thought price was 29606.00 when it actually was 29591.50, the engine may have triggered the close *after* the price had already moved significantly further against the position than the engine's view suggested.

### Reconciling the two views

The most plausible interpretation that makes both views internally consistent:

1. **The system intended to enter Long** — `DebugTrades.Direction='Long'`, `OrderPlaced` recorded with SL **below** entry (29539.1 < 29642.25), which is only correct for a Long.
2. **The broker may have routed the entry differently**, or there was an earlier opposing position that this order "covered + flipped". In other words: the most likely scenario is that **what the system logged as a single `EntryFilled` event was actually the net result of a flip cascade** — and our local recorder collapsed it into one Long fill at the volume-weighted average price.

## 13. Recommended Investigation Steps

In order of priority:

1. **Fix exit-price reconciliation (root cause).** Find the code path that writes `LiveTradeRecords.ExitPrice` / `DebugTrades.ExitPrice` on a ClosePosition flow. The current behavior is "stamp from local price at close-decision time". The correct behavior is "stamp from the broker's `ExecutePrice` on the closing fill (Side=Ask, Disposition=Closing) once it's confirmed." Likely files to inspect:
   - `src/TopStepTrader.Services/Trades/TradeRecordService.vb` — where `ExitPrice` is set
   - `src/TopStepTrader.Core/Interfaces/IExitExecutionService.vb` (newly added on this branch — confirm the contract returns the fill price, not the decision price)
   - The handler that consumes broker fill events on `Disposition=Closing` orders
2. **Backfill recent trades from broker tape.** Because the bug is bidirectional (over- and under-reporting), every trade's PnL since this regression is suspect. Build a one-shot reconciliation utility that pulls broker `ExecutePrice` for each `LiveTradeRecords` row where `ExitOrderId != 0` (or matches by trade-day + contract + entry order) and rewrites the row. Bonus: also rewrite `PnL` and `ExitTime`.
3. **Fix the recorder gap.** `TradeOrderSnapshots`, `TradeFillSnapshots`, and `TradePositionSnapshots` are empty system-wide. If those tables had captured the closing fill's `ExecutePrice`, this bug would have been visible from `LiveTradeRecords.PnL` vs `TradeFillSnapshots.Price` reconciliation. Verify the snapshot-recorder DI registration in `src/TopStepTrader.Services/ServicesExtensions.vb` and the broker-stream hook subscription.
4. **Add a CI-level assertion in `LiveTradeRecords`-write code paths**: on close, refuse to commit a `PnL` value that wasn't derived from a broker-confirmed `ExecutePrice`. This converts the bug class from "silent drift" to "fail loud."
5. **Audit the "P&L Close" trigger condition.** The engine fired close at `UnrealisedPnlDollars = -$84` for trade 1, but the realised loss was −$200. If the threshold is "−$X dollars," the engine's view is currently *under-reading* drawdown by 1–2 ticks worth of latency on a fast-moving market. Consider whether the trigger should be price-based (broker tick) rather than computed from a stale local `UnrealisedPnlDollars`.
